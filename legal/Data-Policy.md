# VIBEITY Data Policy
*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

## 1. About this policy
This policy explains what personal information VIBEITY collects, why, how we protect it, and your rights. BhigC Graphix (trading as VIBEITY) is the “data controller”. We aim to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and guidance from the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

## 2. Information we collect
Information you give us: your name, email address, password (stored securely, never in plain text), gender, what you are here for (relationship or friendship), profession, state and city, bio, username, profile photo and the other details you add.

Content you create: posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, saves, shares, messages, voice notes, files, group photos and group activity.

Choices and settings: privacy setting, notification choices, blocked users, muted and pinned chats, group settings and swipe choices (who you liked, super-liked or passed).

Permissions you allow on your device: camera, microphone, notifications and, if you choose, location. You can turn these off at any time in your browser or phone settings.

Technical information: device and browser type, approximate time of use, and the “online” status shown to other users (based on when you were last active).

Information from others: when someone messages you, comments, shares a post with you, adds you to a group or blocks you.

## 3. How we use your information
To create and run your account, show your profile, suggest people to discover, create matches and friendships, and deliver messages, calls, posts and notifications.

To keep VIBEITY safe: preventing fake accounts, scams and abuse, enforcing our Terms, and responding to reports.

To improve the App, fix problems and understand what features people use.

To contact you about your account, security or important changes. We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Our reasons (legal bases)
Contract: to provide the service you signed up for.

Consent: for optional features such as location, camera, microphone, notifications and contacts. You can withdraw consent at any time.

Legitimate interests: to keep the App safe and working, as long as this does not override your rights.

Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or share information.

## 5. Who can see your information
Public accounts: your profile details, posts and friends list can be seen by other users. Private accounts: only your friends can see your posts and friends list. Your name, username and profile photo may still appear so people can find and request you.

Likes, saves and shares are counted and shown as numbers. Other users cannot see who saved or shared a post.

Messages and calls are visible only to the people in that chat or group. Group chats are visible to all current group members.

View-once photos, videos and voice notes are removed for the recipient after they are opened. People can still screenshot or record what they see, so please share carefully.

## 6. Who we share information with
Service providers who help us run VIBEITY under strict instructions, for example cloud database, authentication and file storage (our backend is hosted with Supabase, with servers in the United Kingdom), website hosting (Vercel) and font delivery (Google Fonts).

Authorities or other parties where required by law, to protect someone’s safety, or to enforce our Terms.

A buyer or successor, if the business is ever sold or reorganised, with the same protections for your information.

We do not sell your data to advertisers or data brokers. If we ever introduce advertising or analytics that need new sharing, we will update this policy first and ask for consent where required.

## 7. Transfers outside Nigeria
Your information may be stored and processed outside Nigeria, including in the United Kingdom. We only use providers that protect data to a standard comparable to Nigerian law and we use contracts and security measures to protect it.

## 8. How long we keep information
Profile and content: until you delete it or your account. After deletion we remove it from live systems within a reasonable period, and backups expire on a regular cycle.

Messages: until you or the other person delete them or the account is deleted. Using “Clear chat” hides messages for you.

View-once media: removed for the recipient once opened.

Safety records: reports, blocks and records of serious violations may be kept for as long as needed to protect users and meet legal duties.

## 9. Keeping your information safe
We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), password protection, access controls and database rules so that people can only reach the data they are allowed to see. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password and never share it.

If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the NDPC as required by law.

## 10. Your rights
Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can: ask to see the personal data we hold about you; ask us to correct it; ask us to delete it; object to or ask us to restrict certain uses; ask for a copy in a portable format; and withdraw consent at any time.

To use these rights, email collinsolukoju@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We may need to verify who you are. We will reply within the time the law allows.

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

## 11. Children
VIBEITY is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you think a child is using VIBEITY, email collinsolukoju@gmail.com and we will act quickly.

## 12. Browser storage
We use your browser’s local storage and similar technology to keep you signed in, remember your settings and make the App work faster. We do not use this storage to track you across other websites. You can clear it in your browser settings, but you may then need to sign in again.

## 13. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as VIBEITY grows. The date at the top shows the latest version. For important changes we will tell you in the App or by email.

## 14. Contact
Questions or requests about your data: collinsolukoju@gmail.com. BhigC Graphix (trading as VIBEITY), Nigeria.
