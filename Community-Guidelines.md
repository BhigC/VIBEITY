# VIBEITY Community Guidelines
*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

## Our promise
VIBEITY is for real people making real connections. These guidelines keep it respectful, safe and fun. Everyone who uses VIBEITY agrees to follow them.

## 1. Be 18 or over, and be real
You must be 18 or older. Use your own name and photos. Do not pretend to be someone else, use fake profiles, or run several accounts.

## 2. Respect every person
Treat people the way you want to be treated. No harassment, bullying, stalking, threats, insults or unwanted repeated messages. If someone says no or stops replying, accept it and move on.

## 3. No hate
Do not attack people because of their tribe, race, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or background. Hate speech and symbols are not allowed.

## 4. Keep it appropriate
No nudity, pornography or sexually explicit photos, videos, voice notes or messages. No sexual content involving anyone under 18, ever. We report this to the authorities.

No graphic violence, gore, or content that encourages self-harm. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust or a local support service.

## 5. Consent and privacy
Do not share anyone’s private photos, messages, phone number, address or other personal information without their permission. Do not screenshot, record or forward private chats, view-once media or calls to embarrass or harm someone.

Do not post photos or videos of other people without their consent.

## 6. No scams, money requests or spam
Do not ask people for money, gifts, airtime, crypto or personal financial details. Do not run investment, romance or “urgent help” scams. No mass messages, fake giveaways or unwanted advertising. Business promotion needs our written permission.

## 7. Stay safe when meeting offline
Meet in a busy public place, tell a friend or family member where you are going, and arrange your own transport. Never send money to someone you have not met. Trust your instincts and leave if you feel unsafe.

## 8. No illegal activity
Do not use VIBEITY to sell or promote illegal drugs, weapons, stolen goods, fake documents, fraud, or any activity that breaks Nigerian law.

## 9. Groups and communities
Group admins and members must follow these guidelines. Do not use groups to harass, spread rumours, share explicit content or recruit for scams. Admins can remove members who break the rules, and we may remove groups that do.

## 10. Reels, comments and images
Post content you have the right to share. Do not copy other people’s work. Comments and comment images must follow the same rules as everything else.

## 11. Report and block
Use Block in any chat to stop a person contacting you. To report a person, post, comment, image or group, email collinsolukoju@gmail.com with the username and what happened. Reports are taken seriously and handled as quickly as we can.

## 12. What happens if rules are broken
Depending on how serious it is, we may remove content, send a warning, limit features, suspend an account or ban it permanently. Serious or illegal behaviour may be reported to the police or other authorities. Decisions can be appealed by emailing collinsolukoju@gmail.com.
